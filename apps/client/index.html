THE BASICS

Let’s move the chains.

  1. Call a play. Choose a pass, inside zone, outside sweep, or draw. Enter snaps the ball. Runs have an automatic handoff to your running back.
  2. Find your receiver. Pull backward from the quarterback and release to throw, or press anywhere on the field, drag to a landing spot, and release. A quick click still throws to that spot or an assisted receiver. F (or the buttons) switches between fast bullet passes and higher, longer lobs. Press 1–4 for assisted receiver throws or 5 for the running back. X throws the ball away. You must throw from behind the blue line; crossing it commits you to a scramble. Passing while moving or under pressure is less accurate.
  3. Take off. Use WASD or arrow keys. After a catch or handoff, the runner follows the play automatically until you steer. Hold Shift to sprint; watch your stamina. Space jukes nearby defenders but will not make you invincible. Let blockers open a lane before accelerating.
  4. Keep the drive alive. Gain 10 yards in four downs. Touchdowns score 7. On fourth down, you can punt.
  5. Play defense. Choose a coverage, then Enter lets Portland snap. Move the highlighted defender with WASD or arrows. Q or Tab switches to a nearby available defender; clicking a defender on your team selects them directly. Space dives at the carrier or sheds a block. A missed dive leaves a short recovery window. Your teammates follow their assignments. With the ball in the air, E briefly raises your hands to swat; R opens a shorter interception window. Move close to the ball and time the action as it arrives. The landing ring and flight countdown help you read the throw. Lobs pass above underneath defenders.
  6. Win the game. First to 21, or lead after four quarters. Portland plays real drives. Touchdowns, punts, interceptions and fourth-down stops change possession and field position. There is a short clock runoff between plays.

On touch screens, use the directional pad, tap a receiver or the numbered pass buttons, hold Sprint, and tap Juke. On defense, use Switch and Tackle, plus Swat and Pick while a pass is in flight. Online 1v1: create or join a room, then both players ready up. You control your own team on offense and defense. The player on offense calls the snap and advances between plays. P opens the lobby during online play; the match continues. Disconnections pause the match for a 45-second reconnect window. Both players can request a rematch after the final whistle.
